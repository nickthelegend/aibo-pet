What is already settled

1

Print the test plate

Two parts, 7 mm tall, about 15 minutes. It proves the printed spline fits your servo before you commit to an eight hour tub.

2

Run the nine plates

One arm segment per plate, so a knock over costs one part rather than three. Sizes and volumes are on the parts page.

3

Drop the electronics in

The tub opens to its full bore, so every board and the speaker lower straight in. The shoulder ring goes on last.
