180° pan, measured honestly

The reference design gears a 9 g servo into a ring gear; through any such reduction a 180 degree servo cannot reach 180 at the table. Direct drive can, so direct drive it is: 1:1 on the spline, wires through a kidney slot with a service loop.

Sandwich links, windowed servo

Flat plates with round caps. The elbow MG996R passes through a window in the inner plate with its tail proud on the face — a 47.6 mm servo does not fit inside a 37.3 mm gap, so 2.0 stops pretending it does. Cross engagement 1.5 mm with 0.3 running clearance, held by the audit.

The cone survives

The head nose is sized to the shade's measured 37.3 mm yoke gap, so the v1 cone mounts unmodified. Every drive interface — horn recess, threaded stub, yoke-screw, clamp bars — carries over from parts that already printed and held.
