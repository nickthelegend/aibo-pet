Questions

Everything worth knowing before you start a print.

Do I need anything besides a printer?⌄

Four MG996R servos, an ESP32-S3, a MAX98357A amp, an INMP441 microphone, a 40 by 20 mm oval driver, a 12 LED WS2812 ring and one Cherry MX switch. No fasteners at all — every bolt, thread and retainer on the lamp is printed. Wire, solder and a dab of CA glue for two trim cups is the rest.

Will it fit my printer?⌄

HOTARU 2.0 is designed against the Bambu P1S: four plates, the largest 237 x 224 mm. The tub is 150 mm across and the cone 88 mm. v1 was cut for the A1 mini's 180 mm bed and its files are still in the repo.

Does anything need supports?⌄

One thing. The shade collar sits at 51 degrees off vertical, which most printers bridge and some will not. Everything else measured clean under an overhang audit that reads the actual mesh rather than trusting a claim.

How long does it take to print?⌄

About 492 cm3 of plastic, roughly 610 g in PLA, across nine plates. The tub is the long one at about eight hours. The small parts plate is under two.

What if my servo is a clone with different dimensions?⌄

Torque goes through a cross slot rather than the horn screws, so clone screw patterns do not matter. If the body differs, edit four numbers in the params file and rebuild.

Can I print the screws too?⌄

They all are now. Every fastener is a printed P6 thumb bolt turning into a printed thread — M6 major, 2 mm pitch, coarse enough to survive a 0.4 mm nozzle, which a real M3 thread is not. The boards sit on printed corner tabs, the disc is held by two printed keys, and nothing metal is left. Computed shear is 1276 N per thread against 7.5 to 15 N of real joint load.

Is the CAD really open?⌄

Yes. Pure Python with two dependencies, no OpenSCAD and no CadQuery. The STLs are in the repo with direct download links that track the main branch.

What state is the project in?⌄

The geometry is audited and the models are complete. Several component dimensions still come from listings rather than calipers, so measure your parts before printing the tub.

Is the firmware done?⌄

No. The mechanical side is finished and audited. The firmware is the next piece of work, which is why the build guide says soon rather than showing you a wiring diagram that does not exist yet.
