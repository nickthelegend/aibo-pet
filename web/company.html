The bottleneck

Per call.
Per clip.
Same problem.

The fee must be smaller than the payment

A price feed charging 0.001 USDC per call, or a contributor paid for one accepted clip, only works if settling costs a fraction of that. Most chains cannot do it at all.

Robots have no corpus to read

Nobody wrote down what opening a jar feels like, so it cannot be crawled. The only way to get it is to pay people to record it, at a scale payroll cannot reach.

Invoices do not fit the unit

You cannot raise a purchase order for a tenth of a cent. Machine work priced per unit needs settlement that happens without a human in the loop at either end.

The engine

Instruments, network,
settlement

Three parts that only work together. Hardware alone is a gadget. A network without instruments collects phone video. A token without either is nothing at all.

Instrument

Our own devices, so we control what is sensed. Vision, inertial and contact, time aligned at the source rather than stitched afterwards.

Capture

Contributors record real tasks in real places. Consent is captured with the data, not asserted about it later.

Settle

Each accepted contribution is recorded on Avalanche with its terms. Provenance and payment are the same record.

Train

Buyers license slices by task, environment and sensor. Failures come back as new capture targets.

The network

Whoever did the work
gets paid for it

An agent that served a call and a person who recorded a demonstration are the same payee to us: something did a unit of work and is owed for it. One rail settles both.

Contribution is a record, not a receipt

Every accepted unit is written with its terms, its author and a hash of the payload. Algorand, because the fee is fixed and sub cent: a network paying out per clip has the same constraint as one charging per call, and variable gas breaks both.

Payment follows usage, not upload

Contributors earn when a buyer licenses a slice their data sits in. It aligns the incentive with quality: volume alone earns nothing if nobody trains on it.

Buyers get provenance, not promises

Every hour licensed carries a verifiable chain back to the person who recorded it and the terms they agreed. That is the part a legal team actually blocks on, and it is the hardest thing for a competitor to retrofit.

Where this honestly stands. The agent layer is live and settling real calls today. Hotaru is shipping, with public CAD, STLs and audits. The wearables are in design, and the data network is the model we are building toward rather than something you can use this week. We would rather say that here than have you find out after signing something.
